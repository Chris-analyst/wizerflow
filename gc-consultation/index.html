What does Wizerflow actually do?

We're a research and scouting consultancy. We watch SAM.gov every day, and when demand shows up in a lane we know, we dig into the attachments, the wage determination, and the incumbent's track record. You get the intel — you decide what to do with it.

Who is this for?

Two sides. Government buyers who want vendors with real capacity. Contractors who want qualified opportunities instead of noise. We do the research both sides would otherwise do alone.

Which services do you cover?

Three main lane families. Facilities and maintenance: janitorial, landscaping, roofing, plumbing + HVAC, light maintenance. Property and lodging: leasing, lodging. Staffing and supplies: staffing, tools and parts. Need something outside those lanes? We run custom searches for consulting clients.

How does the education side work?

We teach what we practice: reading solicitations and scopes, sizing opportunities against real market data, and running the SAM.gov process without losing work to paperwork. Format and scope follow what you need — we agree on both before anything starts.

What does it cost?

We agree on scope and cost on the alignment call, before anything starts. No hidden fees, no per-listing charges.

How do we start?

Book an alignment call. Tell us your lane. We'll tell you what we're seeing move through it — and where you fit.
