What does Wizerflow actually do?

We watch SAM.gov every day. When demand shows up that fits a lane we know, we qualify it and route it to the right performer. You meet the opportunity — not a listings page.

Who is this for?

Two sides. Government buyers who need a vendor with real capacity. And contractors who want qualified opportunities instead of noise. We stand between the two and make the introduction.

Which services do you cover?

Three lane families, seven services. Facilities and maintenance: janitorial, landscaping, roofing, plumbing + HVAC, light maintenance. Property and lodging: leasing, lodging. Staffing and supplies: staffing, tools and parts.

Are you a staffing agency or a broker?

Neither. We're a concierge. We don't sign the contract and we don't do the work. We track the demand, qualify it, and introduce the people who should be talking.

What does it cost?

We agree on scope and cost on the alignment call, before anything starts. No hidden fees, no per-listing charges.

How do we start?

Book an alignment call. Tell us your lane. We'll tell you what we're seeing move through it — and where you fit.
