What does Wizerflow actually do?

We bid on government work and deliver it. As a prime, we bid directly on solicitations from SAM.gov, states, and local agencies. As a sub, we partner with prime contractors and take care of our piece of the work.

Who is this for?

Two groups. Government buyers who want a vendor that reads the whole scope and delivers on it. And prime contractors who need a subcontractor they can hand work to — and not worry about.

Which services do you cover?

Three main lane families. Facilities and maintenance: janitorial, landscaping, roofing, plumbing + HVAC, light maintenance. Property and lodging: leasing, lodging. Staffing and supplies: staffing, tools and parts.

Where do you bid?

SAM.gov for federal work, plus state and local solicitations. When a solicitation falls in one of our lanes, we read the full scope — attachments, deadlines, and all — before we decide to bid.

What makes you different?

We understand the scope of work before we price it, and we manage the deliverables until the job is closed out. The buyer ends up impressed with the results. That's the standard.
