Do I need to be technical?

No. We install it with you in one session. After that, running an evaluation is one command — you paste in the notice, and the report comes back to you.

Where does my data live?

On your computer. The reports, the documents it downloads, and your evaluation history all stay on your machine. Nothing is hosted with us — outside the install session, we never see your solicitations.

03

What does it cost after?

Nothing monthly. You keep paying your own Claude subscription, and you use your own free SAM.gov API key. There is no subscription to us.

04

What if I need help?

The pilot includes 5 business days of email support after your install. That covers setup questions and your first evaluations. If something is genuinely broken, tell us — that's what a pilot is for.
