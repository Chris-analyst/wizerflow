Which computer will you run it on?

Not on a Mac? No problem — apply anyway. Our install guides are Mac-first, but the system runs on Windows and Linux too. We'll confirm your setup on the call, before you pay anything.

Do you have a Claude subscription, or are you willing to start one?

SAM Evaluator runs inside a Claude subscription — that's where the reading happens, on your account. Without one (or the willingness to start one), the system can't run. If that changes, come back — we'd love to have you.

Is your SAM.gov registration active right now?

No problem — apply anyway and tell us in your email. We'll point you in the right direction.
